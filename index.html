The hands

  • C-LO4, 5, 6 in any order — instant win
  • HeadcrackAny pair plus a 6 — instant win
  • TripsThree of a kind — instant win
  • AceAny pair plus a 1, or 1-2-3 — instant loss

Anything else with a pair sets your point, and the odd die is the number to beat. Full rules.

How it plays

The bank rolls first to set a point. You roll to beat it. Higher point takes the money, a tie is a push, and the bank rolls again.

Host your own table and you are the bank, covering every shooter who sits down. Or open a private room and pass the code to your crew. Win enough and you move up: Shorty, Hustler, Baller, Shooter, OG, Legend.
